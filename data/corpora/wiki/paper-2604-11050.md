# Shared Emotion Geometry Across Small Language Models: A Cross-Architecture Study

**Authors:** Jihoon 'JJ' Jeong  
**ArXiv:** 2604.11050  
**Year:** 2026

## Summary

This paper investigates whether emotion vector geometry is universal across small language models (SLMs) or specific to individual architectures. Using 12 SLMs spanning 6 architectural families (1B-8B parameters), the authors extract 21-emotion vector sets and analyze their representational similarity.

## Key Findings

### Representational Universality
- **Shared Geometry**: Five mature architectures show nearly identical 21-emotion geometry (RDM Spearman ρ = 0.74-0.92)
- **Family Alignment**: Within-family pairs show strongest correlation (Llama 3.2 × Llama 3.1: ρ = 0.92)
- **Behavioral Independence**: Models with opposite behavioral profiles (Qwen vs Llama on MTI Compliance) maintain similar emotion representations (ρ = 0.81)

### Maturity Gradient
- **Scale Correlation**: Anisotropy and RDM std strongly anti-correlated with model size (ρ = -0.88, -0.89)
- **[[rlhf]] Effects**: RLHF substantially reshapes immature representations (Gemma-3 1B) but leaves mature ones unchanged
- **Geometric Stability**: Mature families show within-family base × instruct RDM correlations ≥0.92

## Methodological Contributions

### Critical Findings
1. **Anisotropy Reliability**: Models with extreme anisotropy (>0.95) cannot be safely compared using cosine RDM correlation
2. **Backend Equivalence**: TransformerLens and raw HuggingFace hooks produce numerically equivalent representations (cosine = 0.999998)
3. **Method Effect Decomposition**: Previous "comprehension vs generation" dissociation decomposes into four distinct layers including precision effects and cross-experiment bias

## Technical Details

- **Extraction Pipeline**: Unified comprehension-mode extraction with fp16 precision across all models
- **Analysis Method**: Representational similarity analysis on raw cosine RDMs with anisotropy flagging
- **Architectures**: Qwen 2.5, Llama 3.2/3.1, SmolLM2, Mistral 7B, Gemma-3

## Implications

Establishes that emotion vector geometry is a property of language modeling itself rather than specific training pipelines, with important methodological controls for cross-model comparison studies.
