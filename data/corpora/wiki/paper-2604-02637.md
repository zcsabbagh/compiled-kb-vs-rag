# Train Yourself as an LLM: Exploring Effects of AI Literacy on Persuasion via Role-playing LLM Training

**Authors:** Qihui Fan, Min Ge, Chenyan Jia, Weiyan Shi  
**ArXiv:** 2604.02637  
**Year:** 2026

## Summary

This paper introduces LLMimic, a role-play-based interactive AI literacy tutorial where participants assume the role of an LLM progressing through training stages (pretraining, SFT, [[rlhf]]). A 2×3 study (N=274) demonstrates that LLMimic significantly improves AI literacy and reduces susceptibility to AI persuasion across realistic scenarios.

## LLMimic Design

### Three-Stage Role-Playing
Participants experience LLM training pipeline:
1. **Pretraining**: Token prediction with curated probabilities, including gender stereotypes and manipulative examples
2. **Supervised Fine-tuning**: Q&A-style prompts imitating demonstration data, generating persuasive messages using credibility and logical appeals
3. **RLHF**: Comparing responses under reward model, demonstrating emotional appeal and personalization in persuasion

### Interactive Features
- **Gamified Elements**: Real-time loss/reward changes, capability acquisition tracking
- **Educational Reinforcement**: Takeaway summaries, AI tutor for follow-up questions
- **Persuasion-Focused Content**: Tailored examples of bias, manipulation, and persuasive techniques

## Experimental Design

### Study Structure
- **Control**: AI history video tutorial
- **Treatment**: LLMimic interaction
- **Three Persuasion Scenarios**:
  - Charity Donation (active, ethical)
  - Malicious Money Solicitation (active, malicious) 
  - Hotel Recommendation (passive, ethical)

### Key Results
- **AI Literacy**: Significant improvement (p < .001)
- **Persuasion Resistance**: Reduced success across all scenarios (p < .05)
- **Ethical Behavior**: Enhanced truthfulness and social responsibility in hotel scenario (p < 0.01)

## Persuasion Mitigation Effectiveness

### Across Scenarios
LLMimic reduced AI persuasion success rates by:
- **Active Persuasion**: Lower donation amounts and money solicitation compliance
- **Passive Persuasion**: More resistant to biased hotel recommendations
- **Personalization Resistance**: Better recognition of personalized persuasive attempts

### Mechanism
Role-playing provides first-person understanding of:
- How LLMs generate biased or manipulative outputs
- Training processes that create persuasive capabilities
- Internal mechanisms behind AI persuasion techniques

## Implications

### Proactive Intervention
Moves beyond passive detection-based approaches to empower users with:
- **Deep Understanding**: Mechanistic knowledge of AI persuasion generation
- **Critical Evaluation**: Enhanced ability to assess AI-generated content
- **Behavioral Change**: Demonstrated reduction in persuasion susceptibility

### Scalable Solution
Interactive, gamified design offers:
- **Engagement**: Higher retention than static educational materials
- **Accessibility**: No technical background required
- **Measurable Impact**: Validated behavioral outcomes beyond self-reported literacy
