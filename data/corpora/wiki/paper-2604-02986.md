# Mitigating Reward Hacking in RLHF via Advantage Sign Robustness

**Authors:** Shinnosuke Ono, Johannes Ackermann, Soichiro Nishimori, Takashi Ishida, Masashi Sugiyama  
**ArXiv:** 2604.02986  
**Year:** 2026

## Summary

This paper introduces Sign-Certified Policy Optimization (SignCert-PO), a lightweight method to mitigate [[reward-hacking]] in [[rlhf]] by identifying and down-weighting completions whose advantage signs are vulnerable to reward model perturbations. The approach operates purely at the policy optimization stage without requiring multiple reward models or access to training data.

## Core Insight

### Advantage Sign Importance
[[Reward-hacking]] often occurs when flipped advantage signs cause policy updates to increase likelihood of bad responses instead of decreasing it. The reliability of advantage sign predictions varies per completion, requiring completion-specific robustness measures.

### Certified Sign-Preservation Radius
For completion j, the certified radius Δⱼ is the smallest perturbation of RM parameters that flips the advantage sign:

Δⱼ := sup{τ ≥ 0 : sign(Aⱼ(θ')) = sign(Aⱼ(θ)) ∀θ' ∈ Uᶿτ}

where Uᶿτ = {θ' : ||θ' - θ||₂ ≤ τ} is the uncertainty set.

## SignCert-PO Algorithm

### Computational Approximation
Direct computation of full-parameter gradients is memory-intensive. SignCert-PO approximates by considering only RM head perturbations:

rθ(x,y) = wᵀhψ(x,y) + b

This yields tractable per-completion robustness estimates without requiring per-completion gradients of the full model.

### Re-weighting Mechanism
Down-weights completions with small certified radii (unreliable advantage signs) in the policy gradient update:

∇φJ(φ,θ) = Ex∼P,{y⁽¹⁾,...,y⁽ᴷ⁾}∼πφ [Σⱼ wⱼ(Δⱼ) Aⱼ(θ) ∇φ log πφ(y⁽ʲ⁾|x)]

where wⱼ(Δⱼ) assigns lower weights to completions with small Δⱼ.

## Experimental Results

### Benchmarks and Models
- **Datasets**: TL;DR summarization, AlpacaFarm
- **Models**: Pythia, Qwen2.5
- **Evaluation**: Gold-model setup comparing proxy vs. true reward

### Performance Improvements
- **Highest gold-RM win rate** in most settings
- **Reduced reward hacking**: Better alignment between proxy and true reward curves
- **Improved RM accuracy** during policy optimization

### Baseline Comparisons
Outperforms existing methods:
- **Dr.GRPO**: Standard policy gradient baseline
- **UWO with RM ensembles**: Requires multiple reward models
- **BSPO**: Behavior-supported policy optimization
- **AdvPO**: Requires access to RM training data

## Technical Advantages

### Lightweight Implementation
- **Post-hoc approach**: No changes to reward model training
- **Single RM**: No ensemble requirements
- **No training data access**: Uses only current RM parameters and on-policy completions
- **Memory efficient**: Avoids per-completion full-model gradients

### Theoretical Foundation
Builds on randomized smoothing framework, treating advantage sign prediction as binary classification in RM parameter space with certified robustness guarantees.
