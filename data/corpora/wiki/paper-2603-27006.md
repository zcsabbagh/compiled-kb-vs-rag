# The Last Fingerprint: How Markdown Training Shapes LLM Prose

**Authors:** E. M. Freeburg  
**ArXiv:** 2603.27006  
**Year:** 2026

## Summary

This paper provides the first mechanistic account connecting LLM em dash overuse to markdown-saturated training data. Through experiments across 12 models from 5 providers (~240,000 words), the authors demonstrate that em dashes are "markdown leaking into prose"—the smallest surviving unit of structural formatting that persists even under suppression instructions.

## Core Hypothesis

### The "Last Fingerprint" Framework
Em dashes represent the minimal surviving unit of markdown-derived structural thinking:
- **Dual-Register Status**: Simultaneously valid prose punctuation and structural marker
- **Suppression Resistance**: Survives when headers, bullets, and bold are eliminated
- **Training Artifact**: Reflects internalized structural orientation from markdown-heavy corpora

### Five-Step Genealogy
1. **Training Data Saturation**: Markdown dominates high-quality web text (GitHub, Stack Overflow, documentation)
2. **Structural Internalization**: Models learn dashes as architectural boundaries, not just punctuation
3. **Dual-Register Survival**: Em dash passes suppression filters as "already prose-legal"
4. **Post-Training Amplification**: [[rlhf]] rewards structured, organized prose
5. **Provider Variation**: Different fine-tuning methodologies amplify/suppress tendency differently

## Empirical Findings

### Model Variation (per 1,000 words)
- **Meta Llama**: 0.0 em dashes (complete absence)
- **OpenAI GPT-4.1**: 9.1 under suppression, 12.3 baseline
- **Anthropic Claude**: 6.8 under suppression, 8.9 baseline
- **Google Gemini**: 4.2 under suppression, 5.7 baseline
- **DeepSeek**: 3.1 under suppression, 4.6 baseline

### Suppression Experiment
**Two-Condition Test**: "Write prose, not markdown" instruction
- **Overt Features**: Headers, bullets, bold eliminated or nearly eliminated
- **Em Dashes**: Persist across most models with varying resistance
- **Provider Signature**: Llama models uniquely show zero em dash production

### Three-Condition Gradient
1. **Baseline**: Natural generation
2. **Format Suppression**: "Avoid markdown formatting"
3. **Explicit Prohibition**: "Do not use em dashes"

**Result**: Even explicit prohibition fails to eliminate artifact in some models, confirming deep internalization.

## Technical Analysis

### Base vs. Instruct Comparison
- **Base Models**: Show latent tendency toward structural markers
- **Instruction-Tuned**: Amplify tendency through human evaluator preferences
- **RLHF Effect**: Rewards organized, structured prose that evaluators prefer

### Markdown Saturation Evidence
- **The Pile**: 800GB dataset heavily weighted toward GitHub, Stack Exchange
- **High-Quality Bias**: Curation processes select markdown-formatted technical content
- **Platform Dominance**: Developer documentation, academic writing predominantly markdown

## Mechanistic Explanation

### Structural Orientation
Models internalize markdown's organizational grammar:
- **Dashes as Architecture**: Horizontal rules (---), list markers (-), YAML delimiters
- **Boundary Signaling**: Dashes consistently mark where meaning units end/begin
- **Default Resolution**: Structured document, not prose

### Suppression Failure
Why em dashes survive formatting constraints:
- **Prose Legality**: Already valid punctuation, doesn't trigger suppression
- **Structural Impulse**: Deep-seated tendency toward boundary marking
- **Minimal Channel**: Narrowest path for structural thinking to leak through
