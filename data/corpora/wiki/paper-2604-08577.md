# Distributionally Robust Token Optimization in RLHF

**Authors:** Yeping Jin, Jiaming Hu, Ioannis Ch. Paschalidis  
**ArXiv:** 2604.08577  
**Year:** 2026

## Summary

This paper introduces Distributionally Robust Token Optimization (DRTO), combining token-level [[rlhf]] with Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO) to enhance consistency under distribution shifts. DRTO achieves up to 11.83% relative improvement over standard RTO on mathematical reasoning benchmarks while maintaining computational efficiency.

## Core Innovation

### DRTO Framework
Extends Reinforced Token Optimization (RTO) with minibatch-level robustness:
- **Ambiguity Sets**: f-divergence balls around uniform minibatch distribution
- **Robust Objective**: R(θ; ρ) = sup_{Q∈Ω(P_B)} E_{τ_i~Q}[L_i(θ)]
- **Token-Level Credit**: Maintains RTO's advantage over sequence-level methods

### Two Practical Variants

#### KL-DRTO
- **Ambiguity Set**: Ω^{KL}_ρ(P_B) = {Q : D_{KL}(Q||P_B) ≤ ρ}
- **Entropic Risk**: Ψ_η(θ) = (1/η) log E[exp(η L_i(θ))]
- **Softmax Weights**: Q_η(i) = exp(ηL_i(θ))/Σ_j exp(ηL_j(θ))
- **Effect**: Smoothly emphasizes high-loss trajectories through exponential tilting

#### χ²-DRTO
- **Ambiguity Set**: Ω^{χ²}_ρ(P_B) = {Q : D_{χ²}(Q||P_B) ≤ ρ}
- **Tractable Surrogate**: Based on minibatch mean and standard deviation
- **Computational Advantage**: Avoids iterative dual optimization

## Technical Approach

### RTO Foundation
Builds on Reinforced Token Optimization:
- **Token-Level Rewards**: r^{RTO}_h(x, y_{1:h}) combines DPO ratios and terminal rewards
- **Dense Shaping**: β₁ log(π_{dpo}/π_{ref}) - β₂ log(π_θ/π_{ref}) + β₃ 𝟙_{h=H_i} r(x,y)
- **PPO Updates**: Standard clipped surrogate with token-wise advantages

### Robustness Mechanism
- **Minibatch Reweighting**: Adversarial distribution over trajectory indices
- **Worst-Case Emphasis**: Automatically upweights challenging examples
- **Stability Enhancement**: Reduces sensitivity to reward noise and batch composition

## Empirical Results

### Mathematical Reasoning Benchmarks
- **GSM8K**: +9.17% improvement over standard RTO
- **MathQA**: +2.49% improvement
- **Distribution Shifts**: Linguistic variations, symbolic notation changes
- **Consistency**: More stable performance across evaluation conditions

### Computational Efficiency
- **Runtime Overhead**: Minimal additional cost over standard RTO
- **Training Pipeline**: Same infrastructure as base method
- **Scalability**: No architectural changes required

## Theoretical Properties

### Robustness Guarantees
- **Worst-Case Bounds**: Theoretical protection against adversarial reweighting
- **f-Divergence Framework**: Principled uncertainty quantification
- **Convergence**: Maintains optimization properties of base method

### Comparison to Alternatives
- **DrDPO**: Offline preference optimization vs. online token-level training
- **χ²-Preference Optimization**: Sequence-level vs. token-level credit assignment
- **Group-Robust Methods**: Requires group labels vs. automatic robustness

## Applications

### Multi-Step Reasoning
Particularly effective for:
- **Mathematical Problem Solving**: Enhanced consistency across problem variations
- **Logical Reasoning**: Improved robustness to premise reformulations
- **Code Generation**: Better handling of syntax and style variations

### Distribution Shift Scenarios
- **Linguistic Variations**: Different phrasings of same logical content
- **Format Changes**: Notation, structure, presentation differences
- **Domain Transfer**: Robustness across related problem types

## Implementation Details

### Algorithm Integration
```
1. Collect minibatch B = {τᵢ}ⁿᵢ₌₁ under current policy
2. Compute token-wise RTO rewards {r^{RTO}_h}
3. Calculate PPO losses {Lᵢ(θ)}
4. Solve robust optimization: min_η {Ψ_η(θ) + ρ/η}
5. Update policy with robust weights Q*(i)
```

### Hyperparameter Sensitivity
- **Robustness Radius ρ**: Controls conservatism-performance tradeoff
- **Token-Level Coefficients**: β₁, β₂, β₃ from base RTO method
- **Optimization**: Standard PPO hyperparameters maintained
