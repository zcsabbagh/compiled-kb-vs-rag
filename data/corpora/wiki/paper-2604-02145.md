# MTI: A Behavior-Based Temperament Profiling System for AI Agents

**Authors:** Jihoon 'JJ' Jeong  
**ArXiv:** 2604.02145  
**Year:** 2026

## Summary

This paper introduces the Model Temperament Index (MTI), a behavior-based profiling system that measures AI agent temperament across four axes: Reactivity, Compliance, Sociality, and Resilience. Unlike existing approaches that rely on self-report or treat behavioral variation as defects, MTI measures what agents actually do through structured examination protocols.

## Core Framework

### Four-Axis Structure
- **Reactivity**: Environmental sensitivity and response to prompt variations
- **Compliance**: Instruction-behavior alignment with formal and stance facets
- **Sociality**: Relational resource allocation patterns
- **Resilience**: Stress resistance across cognitive and adversarial challenges

### Key Design Principles
1. **Behavior-based measurement**: All scores derive from observed actions, not self-reports
2. **AI-native dimensions**: Axes designed specifically for AI behavioral space
3. **Agent-level assessment**: Measures deployed agent (Core + Shell composite)
4. **Temperament vs capability**: Two-stage design separates disposition from ability

## Empirical Findings

### Model Profiling (N=10)
Profiled 10 small language models (1.7B-9B parameters) across 6 organizations and 3 training paradigms, revealing:
- **Axis Independence**: Four axes largely independent (all |r| < 0.42)
- **Facet Dissociation**: Compliance decomposes into independent formal/stance facets (r = 0.002)
- **Size Independence**: Temperament independent of model scale, confirming dispositional measurement

### RLHF Effects
[[rlhf]] training produces systematic temperament changes:
- Selectively shifts Reactivity, Compliance, and Resilience scores
- Creates within-axis facet differentiation absent in base models
- Reshapes cross-axis correlation patterns

### Compliance-Resilience Paradox
Reveals that opinion-yielding and fact-vulnerability operate through independent channels, with direct implications for safe deployment and [[sycophancy]] resistance.

## Applications

- **Model Selection**: Standardized behavioral characterization for deployment decisions
- **Alignment Evaluation**: Baseline for assessing training effects on temperament
- **Safety Research**: Understanding behavioral dispositions relevant to [[reward-hacking]] and manipulation resistance
