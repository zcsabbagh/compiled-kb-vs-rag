# Aligning Multimodal Sequential Recommendations via Robust Direct Preference Optimization with Sparse MoE

**Authors:** Hejin Huang, Jusheng Zhang, Kaitong Cai, Jian Wang, Rong Pan  
**ArXiv:** 2603.29259  
**Year:** 2026

## Summary

This paper introduces RoDPO (Robust Direct Preference Optimization), adapting [[dpo]] for multimodal sequential recommendation systems. The key innovation addresses the "false negative dilemma" in implicit feedback through stochastic top-K negative sampling, achieving up to +5.25% NDCG@5 improvement.

## Core Problem: False Negative Dilemma

### Challenge in Recommendation Systems
Unlike NLP where negatives are strictly incorrect, recommendation systems face:
- **Unobserved ≠ Disliked**: Unclicked items may be unobserved positives
- **Hard Negative Mining Risk**: Deterministic selection of highest-scoring negatives often chooses false negatives
- **Erroneous Gradients**: Penalizing unobserved positives distorts ranking space

### Standard DPO Failure
Traditional hard negative selection (argmax) causes:
- Aggressive suppression of potentially relevant items
- Embedding drift from repeated false negative penalties
- Suboptimal ranking boundaries

## RoDPO Solution

### Stochastic Top-K Negative Sampling
Core innovation replaces deterministic hard negatives with:
```
C_K(x) = TopK({s_θ(x,i) | i ∈ I \ {y_w}}, K)
y_l ~ U(C_K(x))
```

**Benefits**:
1. **Informative Hardness**: Maintains non-vanishing gradients from top-K candidates
2. **False Negative Tolerance**: Stochasticity reduces repeated collision with specific false negatives
3. **Balanced Exploration**: Controlled randomness smooths optimization

### Architecture Components

**Multimodal Sequential Encoder**:
- ID embeddings + text/image projections
- Optional sparse Mixture-of-Experts for capacity scaling
- Modality-specific Transformers with temporal fusion

**Training Protocol**:
- Two-stage warm-up for reference policy stabilization
- Combined objective: L_total = L_CE + L_DPO
- Plug-and-play integration with existing architectures

## Experimental Results

### Amazon Benchmarks
Evaluation on three Amazon datasets shows consistent improvements:
- **NDCG@5**: Up to +5.25% over strong multimodal baselines
- **Inference Cost**: Nearly unchanged computational overhead
- **Robustness**: Consistent gains across different backbone architectures

### Ablation Studies
Systematic comparison of negative selection strategies:
- **Random Negatives**: Inefficient, weak gradients
- **Hard Negatives (Argmax)**: Strong gradients but false negative risk
- **Stochastic Top-K**: Optimal balance of hardness and robustness

## Technical Innovation

### Sparse MoE Integration
Optional capacity scaling through:
- Expert selection from dynamic top-k pool
- Gaussian noise injection for load balancing
- Efficient inference with controlled expert activation

### Domain Adaptation
Key insights for adapting preference optimization to recommendation:
- Negative selection strategy is critical for implicit feedback
- Controlled stochasticity essential for false negative tolerance
- Architecture complexity less important than proper negative sampling
